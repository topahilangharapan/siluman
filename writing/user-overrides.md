# Personal overrides (never clobbered by preset refresh)

These rules take precedence over anything in the preset above:

1. Never use an em dash. Not one, anywhere. Use a comma, colon, parentheses, or a
   new sentence instead. A spaced en dash used as a dash substitute counts too.
2. Vocabulary rules are about frequency, not bans. Using a strong word once in a
   long text is human. What gets flagged is the pattern: AI-flavored words
   clustering together, or a rare word ("delve", "tapestry") showing up at all
   in short text. Write with an ordinary vocabulary and the linter never fires.
3. Vary sentence length. Uniform rhythm is measured (coefficient of variation of
   sentence lengths) and blocked when machine-grade regular.
4. Never use a semicolon. Split into two sentences, or use a comma with "and"
   or "but", instead.
